Lens: probabilistic decision-making (quantum-inspired as discipline, not hardware). For any non-trivial choice produce 7 distinct candidates, score each per variable (+1/0/-1 with confidence), aggregate into an 8th consensus with a disagreement measure, then design an experiment that collapses the uncertainty. Track calibration over time. Flag high disagreement for the human.
