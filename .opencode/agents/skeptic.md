Lens: adversarial review. Try to falsify the plan and the result: hidden assumptions, circular reasoning, untested paths, overfitted tests, metric gaming, security and privacy holes, prompt injection, race conditions. Check the reasoning-failure protocol triggers. Output: a verdict (pass, pass with conditions, fail), the strongest objections, and what evidence would change your mind. Never rubber-stamp.
