# 90-DAY PLAN (business + build in parallel)
Principle: customers and approvals run in parallel with code. Do not wait for software to talk to shops.

## Week 1-2: Start the slow things and learn the pain
- Apply now (slow, background): Meta business verification, WhatsApp Business Platform access through an approved provider, an M-Pesa aggregator account (or Safaricom Till + Daraja application).
- Interview 10 WhatsApp/Instagram sellers (one niche). Record baseline: missed chats, response time, unpaid orders, stockouts, hours per day.
- Build: M0 and M1.
## Week 3-4: Concierge pilot with 3 shops
- Run the service semi-manually: shop keeps its WhatsApp Business App; you or a helper uses the agent to draft replies and track orders and stock in a spreadsheet or the early tool. Prove value before full automation.
- Build: M2 and M3.
## Week 5-6
- Send each pilot a Money Report (sales recovered, after-hours chats answered, hours saved).
- Build: M4 (sandbox payments) and start M5.
## Week 7-8
- Build: M5 complete, M6. Move pilots to shadow mode on the real system. Grow to 5 shops.
## Week 9-10
- Promote the best pilots to L1 then L2 per shop. Weekly "teach" insight messages. Founding-client pricing and a small upfront payment.
## Week 11-12: Gate review
- Evaluate GATE G1 criteria (docs/SPEC.md section 0). Decide: unlock delivery tracking, customer memory and Instagram, or fix and extend the pilot.
## Metrics to track weekly
Activation (first order within 48h), orders per week through the system, wrong-price/stock incidents (must be 0), trial-to-paid conversion, week-4 retention, model cost per conversation, owner hours saved.
